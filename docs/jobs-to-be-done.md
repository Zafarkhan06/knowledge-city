# Jobs to be done

A job is what the person needs to get done whether or not this app exists, so none of these mention the app or any of its features. Roles are defined in [app-roles.md](app-roles.md).

J-3 belongs to both roles because both genuinely have it. Every other job belongs to one role.

## J-1 (Bill Payer)

When the bill arrives and the group has agreed to share it equally, I want to know how much each person owes including the tip without doing the sums in my head, so I can collect everyone's money before people leave the table.

## J-2 (Bill Payer)

When I've paid the whole bill and my friends are paying me back, I want to make sure everyone's shares add up to exactly what I paid, so I can get all my money back without anyone arguing over a leftover cent.

## J-3 (Bill Payer, Group Organiser)

When I'm working out who owes what in a hurry, I want to know straight away if one of my numbers is wrong or makes no sense, so I can fix it before I ask anyone to pay the wrong amount.

## J-4 (Bill Payer)

When the numbers change after I've worked out who owes what (someone leaves early, the group agrees a different tip, or another bill arrives), I want to redo the sums quickly without mixing old and new numbers, so I can keep up with the group.

## J-5 (Group Organiser)

When people in my group ordered very different amounts, I want to have each person pay for what they had plus a fair part of the tip, so I can collect exactly what the bill comes to without anyone paying for someone else's meal.

## Jobs considered and not served by this product

- **Deciding how much to tip.** This is real, but it is a social and local-custom decision, not arithmetic. The person brings their own tip percentage.
- **Getting friends to actually send the money.** This needs contacts, payments or messaging, which means accounts and network calls, and both are out of scope.
